## Brand & Style

This design system expresses enterprise rigor, institutional trust, and forensic authority for high-stakes IT audit and governance certification learning. It serves executive learners, risk analysts, and systems auditors who expect the sobriety and precision of bank-grade infrastructure rather than the gamified tropes of consumer edtech.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses **Corporate Modern** structure with **Precision High-Assurance Technical Minimalism**. Visual hierarchy is governed by strict mathematical rhythm, razor-sharp hairline dividers, deep marine structural surfaces, and clinical contrast. The emotional posture is unyielding, validated, and prestigious—commemorating institutional mastery through disciplined utility.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of sovereign security and compliance validation:

- **Primary (`#0A192F`)**: Deep Institutional Navy anchors core navigation bars, executive headers, primary CTA states, and high-security containment shells.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` / `#059669`)**: Audit Pass Emerald represents verified compliance, passing diagnostic thresholds, validation seals, and active session integrity.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B`)**: ISACA Pedigree Gold highlights milestone certifications, industry honors, critical warnings, and timer indicators without devolving into visual alarmism.
- **Neutral (`#334155`)**: Tech Slate governs primary body typography, meta descriptions, structural icons, and muted control chrome.
- **Canvas & Card (`#F8FAFC`, `#FFFFFF`)**: Clinical ice and pure white surfaces provide an immaculate, glare-free ground for rigorous multi-hour study sessions.
- **Dividers (`#E2E8F0`)**: 1px structural outlines delineate every content partition, preserving organizational boundaries without reliance on heavy cast shadows.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the structured architectural geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for section headers with the neutral, exhaustive legibility of **Inter** for all narrative prose, audit guidelines, and interface controls.

A mandatory feature of this system is **tabular numerical alignment (`font-feature-settings: 'tnum' 1`)** across all exam diagnostic displays, timer components, pass probability gauges, domain score cards, and telemetry grids. This prevents layout jitter during real-time countdown updates and aligns numerical columns cleanly across high-density tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform is constructed on an 8pt base unit grid using a 12-column fluid containment model on desktop systems, transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile viewports.

- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12 columns, 24px gutters, maximum container width capped at 1440px for reading comfort during extensive mock-exam analysis. Side panels (telemetry, session info) pin to a 320px fixed rail.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns, 20px gutters, dynamic collateral sidebars fold into an off-canvas drawer or stack below primary review panes.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px outer margin. Data tables switch to stacked summary cards; telemetry figures retain fixed column-width tabular alignment.
- **Density Profile**: High operational density inside active exam screens (tight 8px gaps between option items) paired with relaxed 24px-40px spacing in analytical dashboards.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating, decorative drop shadows. Visual depth is established purely through **tonal layering and crisp structural borders**, reflecting the uncompromising solidity of bank-grade governance software.

1. **Base Tier (Ground)**: Light canvas (`#F8FAFC`) with structural 1px borders (`#E2E8F0`).
2. **Elevated Surfaces (Modules & Cards)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) framed in 1px `#E2E8F0`. When an element requires tactile emphasis or hover response, it utilizes a hairline tint (`#CBD5E1`) and a subtle, high-spread shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
3. **Forensic Overlays**: Dynamic security watermarks (student name, IP address, timestamp, unique hash) render continuously across secure video players and Canvas-rasterized document viewers using SVG text paths at `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` on light documents and `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.12)` in dark-mode video viewports.
4. **Command Layer**: Sticky status ribbons, domain headers, and timer heads use `#0A192F` with border-bottom hairlines (`#1E293B`), establishing authoritative permanence at the top of the viewport.

## Shapes

The design uses a **Soft (`1`)** shape language (`0.25rem` / `4px` baseline) to maintain sharp, geometric, and institutional order. Large, overly playful border radii are strictly avoided.

- **Buttons, Form Inputs, Badges**: `0.25rem` (4px). Clean, crisp corners that project precision engineering.
- **Cards, Audit Containers, Video Frames**: `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`). Controlled softness that separates macro containers from micro interactions.
- **Dividers & Metric Separators**: Continuous 1px unrounded lines aligned flush to grid columns.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#0A192F` background with `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` radius, 12px 20px padding. Hover: `#1E293B`. Focus: 2px offset ring in `#10B981`.
- **Verified Action (Pass / Submit)**: Solid `#059669` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Hover: `#10B981`.
- **Secondary**: `#FFFFFF` background with 1px border in `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. Hover: `#F8FAFC`.
- **Pedigree / Award Highlight**: Dark Navy surface with 1px border in `#D97706` and `#F59E0B` text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status (Compliant / Passed)**: Subdued emerald background (`#ECFDF5`), `#059669` text, 1px border `#A7F3D0`, `4px` radius, uppercase `0.75rem` text with `letter-spacing: 0.04em`.
- **Status (Under Review / Warning)**: Subdued amber background (`#FFFBEB`), `#D97706` text, 1px border `#FDE68A`.
- **Domain Metadata**: `#F1F5F9` background, `#334155` text, hairline border `#E2E8F0`.

### Cards & Container Panels
- White `#FFFFFF` card surfaces wrapped in a crisp 1px `#E2E8F0` border. Headers feature an institutional bottom border divider separating metadata (e.g., Domain Weight, Time Expended) from core question or analytic content.

### Inputs & Form Elements
- **Text Inputs**: Height 40px, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, 4px radius. Focus state swaps border to `#0A192F` with a subtle 1px ring in `#0A192F`. Placeholder in `#94A3B8`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 16px square (checkbox) or circle (radio), 1px border `#94A3B8`. Selected state fills with `#0A192F` and renders a crisp `#FFFFFF` checkmark or dot.

### Telemetry & Diagnostic Elements
- **Score Gauges & Radar Metric Trackers**: High-contrast tabular numerical readouts accompanied by 4px track progress indicators with `#10B981` fills on `#E2E8F0` tracks.
- **Timer Module**: Fixed floating or embedded banner with tabular monospaced digits, shifting from Tech Slate (`#334155`) to ISACA Amber (`#D97706`) below 10% remaining duration.
- **Secure Watermarked Viewers**: Canvas-enclosed reader pane bounded by a 1px `#1E293B` shell, disabling user text-selection styling and forcing hardware-accelerated watermarking layers.
