Programme overview

The Certified Information Systems Auditor (CISA) credential is the world-renowned standard for professionals who audit, control, monitor, and assess an organization's information technology and business systems.

Our rigorous 10-week cohort is specifically structured around the official ISACA CISA Review Manual (28th Edition). Participants work through foundational audit standards, technical systems analysis drills, and simulated proctored practice exams with exhaustive rationale reviews.

Frequently asked questions

How long do I have access to the learning materials?

Your study licence remains active throughout the 10-week cohort, plus an additional 90 days following cohort completion, plus a 30-day exam grace period (total 120 days post-cohort).

Are the official ISACA exam registration fees included?

No. The tuition fee covers all A.J. Silicon lectures, protected slides, recorded sessions, and practice exam simulations. Candidate exam registration must be completed directly on the ISACA international portal.

Can I switch between Physical and Virtual attendance?

Yes. Physical registrants are welcome to attend virtually via Zoom whenever required. All class recordings are uploaded to the secure student classroom within 24 hours.
