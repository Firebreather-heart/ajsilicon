These Terms of Service govern your access to the A.J. Silicon Learning Platform, training programmes, and associated professional assessment resources. By creating an account or registering for any certification syllabus, you agree to comply with and be bound by these institutional terms.

2. User Registration & Eligibility

Enrolment in professional certification preparatory programmes requires candidates to provide accurate corporate or professional credentials during account creation. 3. Tuition Fees & Settlement

All programme fees are quoted in Nigerian Naira (₦) and must be paid in full prior to cohort onboarding or syllabus unlock. Confirmed electronic transactions processed through approved payment gateways constitute legally binding financial commitments for the selected training window.

All training slide decks, recorded lectures, diagnostic exam mock banks, and instructional case studies remain the exclusive intellectual property of A.J. Silicon. Candidates participating in diagnostic mock examinations must maintain strict compliance with international IT audit codes of professional ethics. 6. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

These Terms shall be interpreted and governed in accordance with the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with platform usage that cannot be settled amicably shall be submitted to arbitration in Lagos State, Nigeria.

Privacy Policy

1. Regulatory Framework & Data Controller

A.J. Silicon operates as a registered data controller adhering rigorously to the principles set out in the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA). We process all personal information lawfully, transparently, and with strict respect for the fundamental rights of all registered learners and institutional clients.

2. Personal Information Collected

We collect professional identification data including your full legal name, work email address, telephone number (+234), employer details, and certified exam attempt histories. Payment transactions are handled directly through licensed Nigerian settlement gateways and payment card details are never stored on our operational servers.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

Your personal data is processed to administer cohort participation, issue verifiable professional certificates, and provide accredited CPE and ICAN credit records. In accordance with the NDPA, processing is grounded either in contractual necessity for training delivery or compliance with statutory reporting duties.

4. Data Subject Rights Under the NDPA

Under the NDPA, candidates possess statutory rights to request access, rectification, portability, or erasure of their personal information stored on our platforms. Requests to exercise data subject rights are acknowledged within forty-eight hours and fulfilled without undue delay through our governance office.

5. Security Safeguards & Retention Schedules

We employ enterprise encryption standards, role-based access controls, and multi-factor verification mechanisms to safeguard stored student records against unauthorised disclosure. Academic records and certificate verification datasets are retained in accordance with Nigerian educational record standards for seven years.

6. Data Protection Officer (DPO) Contact

For inquiries regarding personal data processing or to lodge a formal compliance inquiry under the NDPA, contact our designated Data Protection Officer at dpo@ajsilicon.com. Our regulatory team reviews all notices in coordination with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) guidelines.

Content Licence

Version: 1.8 Last updated: 05 Oct 2026 Scope: Educational & Instructional Assets

Contents

  • 1. Grant of Individual User Licence
  • 2. Term of Access & Expiry Conditions
  • 3. Prohibited Exploitation & Sharing
  • 4. Diagnostic Practice Banks & Syllabi
  • 5. Licence Revocation & Infringement Remedies

1. Grant of Individual User Licence

Subject to confirmed tuition payment, A.J. Silicon grants the enrolled candidate a revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable personal licence to view designated instructional syllabus modules. This licence is strictly limited to the individual candidate and cannot be assigned, sublicensed, or shared across multiple corporate employees.

2. Term of Access & Expiry Conditions

Active licence duration extends for the exact duration of the enrolled cohort plus a ninety-day post-cohort study window with an additional thirty-day exam buffer. Upon conclusion of the designated access period, access to protected lectures and slides ceases automatically unless an extension licence is acquired.

3. Prohibited Exploitation & Sharing

The licensee shall not record, broadcast, duplicate, or capture platform instructional materials through screen-capture utilities or third-party devices. Facilitating group screenings or commercial resale of proprietary course notes constitutes a severe contractual violation resulting in immediate licence forfeiture.

4. Diagnostic Practice Banks & Syllabi

Diagnostic questions, distractor explanations, and simulated domain mock exams are licensed solely for individual certification readiness assessment. Systematic harvesting, indexing, or redistributing question items undermines educational evaluation integrity and constitutes a breach of copyright.

5. Licence Revocation & Infringement Remedies

A.J. Silicon reserves the right to terminate this licence immediately upon discovering evidence of unauthorised dissemination or account credential compromise. Upon termination, all privileges to access online instructional content terminate without entitlement to partial fee refund.

Refund Policy

Version: 2.0 Last updated: 05 Oct 2026 Terms: Commercial Tuition & Cohort Fees

Contents

  • 1. General Refund Principles
  • 2. Withdrawal Prior to Cohort Commencement
  • 3. Post-Commencement & Non-Refundable Items
  • 4. Cohort Transfer & Deferral Provisions
  • 5. Refund Processing Timeline & Method

1. General Refund Principles

A.J. Silicon maintains fair, transparent financial policies balancing institutional seat allocation commitments with professional learner circumstances. Because physical and virtual cohort capacities are strictly limited to ensure instructor-led quality, cancellations impact class operational viability.

2. Withdrawal Prior to Cohort Commencement

Written cancellation notices submitted at least fourteen calendar days prior to scheduled cohort start qualify for an eighty-five percent refund of tuition fees paid. Cancellation requests submitted between thirteen and seven calendar days before cohort inception qualify for a fifty percent refund subject to an administrative processing charge.

3. Post-Commencement & Non-Refundable Items

No refunds shall be issued once cohort orientation has commenced or once digital learning materials and slide modules have been accessed. Dedicated 1-day workshops, examination voucher payments, and corporate custom training engagements are strictly non-refundable once booked.

4. Cohort Transfer & Deferral Provisions

Enrolled candidates experiencing corporate scheduling conflicts may request a single cohort deferral to the next subsequent intake without tuition penalty. Deferral applications must be lodged in writing at least five business days before cohort commencement and remain valid for six months.

5. Refund Processing Timeline & Method

Approved refunds are processed via the originating Nigerian bank account or electronic card channel within ten business days of formal administrative confirmation. International payment transfer surcharges and bank reconciliation deductions remain the responsibility of the applicant.
