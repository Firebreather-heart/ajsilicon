Enterprise IT Risk Assessment Matrix & RACI Framework

Section 2.4: Roles, Responsibilities, and IT Steering Committee Alignment

REF: CISA-D2-WP04
VER: 2026.2 (Virtual)

In executing IT audit mandates aligned to ISACA standards, the information systems auditor must verify that strategic IT goals are directly mapped to organizational risk tolerance through an empowered IT Steering Committee. Audit testing procedures require substantive inspection of meeting minutes, committee charter approvals, and verified RACI allocations for all high-impact information asset changes.

The following RACI matrix details the baseline segregation of duties (SoD) required under standard IT compliance reviews for enterprise asset governance:

Control ID Governance Activity Board / Risk CIO / IT Lead IT Auditor Compliance Criteria
GV-01.1 IT Strategic Plan Sign-off A R I Annual board review minutes
GV-01.2 Risk Appetite Calibration A C I Quantitative appetite statement
GV-02.1 Critical SoD Conflict Reviews I R C Quarterly ERP entitlement matrix
GV-03.4 Independent Controls Testing I C R / A Unimpeachable audit workpaper file
Audit Workpaper Explanatory Key:
R: Responsible
A: Accountable
C: Consulted
I: Informed

Audit Assertion: The auditor must corroborate whether single points of failure exist in IT project sign-offs without documented Board Steering Committee escalation.

Exam Focus Note (Domain 2 Practice):

When an exam scenario presents a conflict between operational IT management and internal audit findings regarding strategic alignment, the IT Steering Committee is the designated primary authority for final escalation.
